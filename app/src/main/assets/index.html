SMS & Verification Services 4 Questions
How quickly do I receive the SMS verification code?
SMS verification codes typically arrive within 2 to 5 seconds. If no SMS arrives within 5 minutes, you can refresh or cancel for an automatic full refund.
Are these numbers real SIM cards or VoIP?
We operate real physical cellular SIM card routes from global mobile carriers, guaranteeing maximum acceptance on WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, Tinder, and strict platforms.
What happens if an SMS code is delayed or does not arrive?
If the SMS code does not arrive within the 5-minute timer window, you can cancel the order directly from your Active Purchases dashboard. Your payment or wallet credits will be immediately refunded with zero cancellation fees.
Can I receive multiple OTPs on the same virtual number?
Temporary lines lock single service for 5 minutes with a single guaranteed OTP code. For multiple OTPs over 24 hours or 7 days, select our Rental Line or Premium Line options in Marketplace.
Payments & Wallet Top-Up 3 Questions
How does the UPI payment & UTR verification work?
After clicking "Proceed to Pay", scan the UPI QR code or pay via PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay, or BHIM. Submit your 12-digit UTR reference number to unlock your virtual number or wallet balance immediately.
Does my wallet balance expire?
No, your wallet balance and referral earnings never expire. You can keep credits in your account and use them whenever you need to activate virtual numbers.
Are refunds instant if a number fails to receive SMS?
Yes! Refund processing is 100% automated. If you cancel an unfulfilled number activation, your wallet balance is restored immediately without requiring support ticket intervention.
Account & Referral Rewards 3 Questions
How does the Refer & Earn 10% lifetime rewards program work?
When you invite friends using your unique referral link or code, your friend gets an instant ₹50 welcome bonus! Whenever they rent virtual numbers or top up their wallet, you automatically earn 10% cash credits directly in your wallet.
How do I claim my ₹50 welcome bonus?
Simply register or sign in to your Virtual Hub account. If you joined using a referral code, the ₹50 welcome bonus is automatically credited to your wallet balance upon registration!
Is my personal data and activation history private?
Yes! We strictly respect user privacy and security. We do not link your real personal identity to temporary phone activations, and all data transmissions are encrypted.
Technical & Power Tools 3 Questions
Can I rent multiple virtual numbers in bulk?
Yes! Use the multi-select checkboxes in the Marketplace or the "Bulk Operations" toolbar to select multiple numbers across different countries and platforms, then checkout in a single UPI transaction.

When enabled in your Active Purchases dashboard, our background engine polls incoming SMS streams every 3 seconds and automatically speaks your 6-digit OTP code using web speech synthesis as soon as it arrives!
